# Landing Page Design Notes

## Final Design Summary

Selected direction: **Modern polished institutional homepage**.

The final design keeps Kisii University's existing crest, campus photography, blue/orange brand palette, and admissions-focused homepage flow, while improving the first-viewport hierarchy with a clearer hero, applicant action panel, safe trust facts, and a visible academic pathway.

## Why This Direction Won

The modern polished direction scored strongest overall because it best balances:

- Product accuracy and audited backend/frontend constraints
- Clear prospective-student conversion path
- Strong desktop and mobile hierarchy
- Brand continuity with a more refined visual system
- Feasibility for future implementation in the existing Next.js/Tailwind structure

## Key Sections

- Announcement strip for admissions guidance
- Header/navigation with KSU brand and primary Apply Now CTA
- Hero with campus photography and clear value proposition
- Applicant action panel: Admissions Guide, Programmes, Student Portal
- Safe fact row: Public University, 8 Schools, Chartered 2013, Founded 1965
- Find your path: Explore Schools, Choose Programme, Apply
- Schools grid using audited school labels
- Admissions Guide CTA card
- Teaching, Research, and Community storytelling cards
- Research & Innovation plus News & Events
- Footer with supported site areas and audited contact details

## Visual Guidance

Colors:
- Primary blue: `#2563EB` / `#3B82F6`
- Accent orange: `#F97316`
- Deep navy for footer and emphasis panels
- White cards with slate text

Typography:
- Serif display style for the hero headline only
- Sans-serif UI typography for navigation, cards, body copy, and CTAs

CTA hierarchy:
- Primary: `Apply Now`
- Secondary: `Explore Programmes`
- Supporting: `View Admissions Guide`, `View programmes`, `View all schools`

## Mobile Guidance

Mobile is intentionally reordered around applicant tasks:

- Announcement and compact header first
- Hero CTAs near the top
- Applicant action panel immediately after hero
- Trust facts before detailed content
- Vertical academic journey
- Compact schools grid without programme counts
- Footer as accordion-style groups

## Accessibility Notes

The final assets use high-contrast text over image overlays, large tap targets, readable card text, strong CTA distinction, and generous spacing. Text is not intentionally placed over busy imagery without a dark overlay.

## Product Truthfulness Constraints

The design avoids unsupported rankings, testimonials, fake partner strips, fake metrics, fake deadlines, fake payments, and fake application dashboard states. `Apply Now` is treated as an admissions-guide/process CTA, not a confirmed online application submission flow.

Allowed facts used:

- Public University
- 8 Schools
- Founded 1965
- Chartered 2013
- `info@kisiiuniversity.ac.ke`
- `P.O. Box 408-40200, Kisii, Kenya`

## Implementation Notes

No implementation was performed. These are visual design assets only. A future implementation can build from existing homepage primitives: public header, announcement bar, hero, card grids, CTA cards, school/programme data, news/events lists, and footer components.

## Self-Evaluation

Final desktop and mobile assets passed all critical checks and scored at least 4/5 in every rubric category.
